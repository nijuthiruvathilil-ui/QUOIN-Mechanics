Quoin Mechanics is a mechanical engineering consultancy founded on the belief that rigorous thinking and hands-on craft are not mutually exclusive.

Engineering from first principles

Quoin Mechanics was founded with a clear mandate: bring the same discipline that governs structural analysis and systems design to the consulting process itself. No shortcuts, no approximations — just rigorous, repeatable engineering.

Over the years we have worked across product development, prototyping, 3D printing, and 2D drafting — partnering with startups, manufacturers, and research institutions to turn complex briefs into working solutions.

Our name reflects our philosophy. A quoin is the cornerstone that locks an arch in place — the element that makes everything else hold. That is the role we aim to play in every project we take on.

Case studies

Projects & Work

A selection of engineering challenges we've solved — from early-stage concept through to delivered systems.

01 · Robotics · 3D Modeling · 2018

Design of palm assembly for “Vyommitra” ISRO's Half Humanoid Robot

Designed palm assembly having kinematic links and actuators embedded. The project was a challenging one with complex mechanism and aesthetics in balance.

  • Kinematic modelling
  • End-effector design and tooling
  • CAD Modeling
02 · Structural Analysis · Moving Tower · 2021

Movable Camera-Tower Structure — Kerala Cricket Association

Design and development of a movable camera-tower structure which is 11 meter high and weighs over 5 ton at SD College, Alappuzha for Kerala Cricket Association. The tower moves on a rail track and can align to any of the 9 pitches. FE analysis was conducted for different load conditions.

  • FEA model build and mesh validation
  • Static and fatigue load case analysis
  • Weld joint redesign and documentation
  • CAD modelling and BoM preparation
03 · Product Development · 3D Modeling & 3D Printing · 2018

Prosthetic Face Design for “Vyommitra” ISRO's Half Humanoid

Design and development activities of prosthetic face design of ISRO's Vyommitra. The task was to develop a human-like face and its mold in CAD and 3D print the same.

  • Character design & modeling
  • 3D-printed prototype iterations
  • Mold generation & 3D printing
04 · Systems Engineering · Decentralized Biodiesel Plant · 2023

Indhan — The Decentralized Small Scale Biodiesel Plant

Quoin Mechanics delivered a comprehensive turnkey solution for Indriyam Biologics' Indhan project — a fully automated, decentralized small-scale biodiesel plant. Scope covered complete plant architecture, custom automation, PLC programming, and facility layout design.

  • Structural and thermal design
  • Piping system design
  • Layout design
  • PLC programming
05 · 2D Drafting · Industrial · 2018

2D Drafting Package — Automatic Spinning Machine

Produced a complete manufacturing drawing package for an automatic coir spinning machine. Drawings were prepared to BIS standards and delivered ready for third-party fabrication and inspection.

  • General arrangement and detail drawings
  • GD&T tolerancing to BIS
  • Bill of materials and parts list
  • Fabrication-ready drawing package
06 · Prototyping · Medical Device · 2023

Rapid Prototyping — Medical Equipment

Delivered four iterative prototypes for a medical diagnostic device ergonomically designed considering the patient's and doctor's perspective. Each cycle refined geometry, material, and assembly method based on user feedback, culminating in a production-ready design.

  • Concept modelling and DFM review
  • Multiple prototype iterations
  • Material and finish specification
  • Production-ready CAD and BOM
